Confirmed ≠ Potential
Use “Testing confirmed…” only for observed behaviour; use “could” for possible consequences. Never assume a real breach or loss.
Choose a justified rating
Impact = specific business harm. Likelihood = prerequisites, reachability, effort and existing controls. Justify the risk rating separately.
Two writing formats
Executive Summary is for management. Detailed Finding is for evidence, reproduction and engineering.
